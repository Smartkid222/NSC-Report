Welcome to Nifty Steps

Nurturing Character, Academic Distinction & Global Leadership

Since our founding in 2008, our singular goal has been to provide an environment where children discover their potential and exceed global academic expectations. Every pupil is known, valued, and challenged to take the next "nifty step" forward.

We combine the depth of the Nigerian National Curriculum with modern international STEM methodologies, ensuring our students excel in WAEC, NECO, Cambridge IGCSE and JAMB examinations while cultivating empathy, discipline and critical thinking.

Our primary programme combines Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) principles with the Nigerian National Primary Curriculum. We encourage curiosity, fundamental mathematical concepts, language fluency and creative arts.
